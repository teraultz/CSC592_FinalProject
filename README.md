## Office Paper Toss
Office Paper Toss is originally a mobile app game in which the player, acting as an office worker, attempts to throw crumpled paper balls into a trash bin while navigating various obstacles across levels. My project reimagines this concept in the world of virtual reality, giving players a more immersive and interactive way to experience the familiar challenge.
